## About

This project began in May 2021, reached a state of "nearly complete" in 2024,
and was put into open beta in December 2025. More content is planned, but for
now the development focus is on balance, polish, and quality of life upgrades.

The game is made with [Zig v0.14](https://ziglang.org) and `SDL2`.

### History

Oathbreaker was originally a very different game. The mechanics revolved around
"movement patterns", a hardcoded list of steps the player would take to unlock
abilities or practice stealth — mainly based on ideas from
[Ironwood](https://push.cx/ironwood-a-roguelike-game-in-7-days), a 7DRL by Peter
Harkins. For example: unleashing a fireball might require moving left, attacking
left, moving left, and moving down.

Unfortunately, by 2023 it was clear that these mechanics didn't fit well with
the kind of stealth game that I wished to create. The movement pattern idea was
completely scrapped, although I hope to explore the idea in the future.

I did attempt something *similar* in [Ancient
Rage](https://kiedtl.itch.io/ancient-rage), although it doesn't seem to have
worked out.
